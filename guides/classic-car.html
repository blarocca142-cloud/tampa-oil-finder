Classics reward careful fluid choices: flat-tappet cams, older seal materials, long storage, and non-modern transmission designs all matter. Treat this as a category checklist, then verify suitability.

Motor oil

Some vintage engines benefit from oils formulated with classic-appropriate additive approaches (for example higher ZDDP discussions in enthusiast literature). Whether a modern synthetic is appropriate depends on the engine build and seal condition — confirm product guidance on AMSOIL.com and with your mechanic.

Gear lube

Differentials and manual gearboxes often need gear oil in specific GL ratings and viscosities. Category: gear lube — verify weight and limited-slip needs.

Automatic transmission (if equipped)

Older automatics may specify fluids that differ from modern ATF. Do not assume a current multi-vehicle ATF is correct without verification.

Grease & storage

Chassis grease and storage practices (fuel, oil film, fogging where appropriate) help classics sit between drives. Product selection belongs on AMSOIL.com.
