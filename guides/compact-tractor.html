Compact tractors can combine a diesel engine, shared transmission and hydraulic reservoir, axles, and implements. Use this overview to map the product categories before confirming the exact tractor application on AMSOIL.com.

Diesel engine oil

Choose a heavy-duty diesel engine-oil category that meets the tractor maker's viscosity and performance requirements. Consider dust, load, idling, and seasonal use when following the service schedule.

Transmission and hydraulic fluid

Many compact tractors use a shared transmission/hydraulic system, while others separate components. The correct universal-tractor or hydraulic category is model-specific; verify it before service.

Axles, differentials, and gearboxes

Front axles, final drives, and gearboxes may need gear-lubricant categories distinct from the central hydraulic system. Confirm every compartment separately.

Filters and implements

Engine, hydraulic, and fuel filters have different jobs and fitments. Implement gearboxes and grease points also need their own maintenance categories and intervals.

Reminder

  • Do not assume all tractor compartments use the same fluid
  • Use the tractor manual and official lookup for capacities and specifications
