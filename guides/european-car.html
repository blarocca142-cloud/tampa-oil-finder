European passenger cars often require oils and fluids that meet specific ACEA sequences and OEM approvals. Matching the manual matters more than brand familiarity alone.

Motor oil

Look for European-formula or ACEA-oriented synthetic motor oil categories that can meet your vehicle’s listed specifications. Viscosity (for example mid-SAPS vs other formulations) must match the manufacturer’s requirement — confirm on AMSOIL.com for your VIN/year/make/model path.

Transmission fluid

Dual-clutch, conventional automatic, and manual gearboxes each have unique fluid specs. Category: OEM-spec ATF / transmission fluid — verify before purchase.

Coolant

European coolants are frequently silicate or OAT chemistry specific. Do not mix incompatible coolants. Confirm chemistry via OEM guidance and product details on AMSOIL.com.

Filters

Oil filter fitment should come from the official vehicle lookup.
