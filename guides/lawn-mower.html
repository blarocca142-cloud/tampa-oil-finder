Walk-behind mowers, riders, and many outdoor power tools use air-cooled four-stroke engines. Keeping fluid choices simple — and verified — protects warranties and reliability.

Four-stroke engine oil

Most mowers need a small-engine or four-stroke motor oil in the viscosity the OEM recommends (often different from passenger-car defaults in extreme heat). Confirm the category and viscosity on AMSOIL.com for your equipment when available, or follow the engine manufacturer’s chart.

Grease

Deck spindles, wheels, and linkages may call for periodic grease. Category: grease — use products suited to outdoor equipment.

Seasonal fuel care

Fuel stabilizer / additive categories can help with storage; always follow label directions and verify the product on AMSOIL.com.

Two-stroke note

If your equipment is two-stroke, oil mixing ratios and oil type are critical — do not use four-stroke oil in a two-stroke sumpless design. Verify on AMSOIL.com.
