Diesel pickups work hard: towing, heat, soot loading, and long intervals between services. This page outlines product categories owners often review — not exact part numbers.

Engine oil

Look for heavy-duty or diesel-rated synthetic motor oil categories that match your engine’s required viscosity and API / OEM diesel specifications. Always confirm the exact product on AMSOIL.com for your year, make, and model.

Transmission

Whether you have an automatic or manual, transmission fluid must match the OEM specification. Use the official lookup rather than guessing from a bottle label alone.

Differentials & transfer case

Front and rear differentials (and transfer cases on 4×4 trucks) often need gear lube in specific weights, sometimes with limited-slip considerations. Category: differential / gear lube — verify fitment online.

Filters

Oil filters (and by-pass filtration where applicable) should be confirmed by vehicle lookup for correct fitment.
