Marine outboard fluids — category starter guide

Outboard engines face water exposure, changing loads, and long storage periods. Use this checklist to identify the product categories to review, then confirm the exact application on AMSOIL.com.

Two-stroke or four-stroke engine oil

First identify the engine design. Two-stroke and four-stroke outboards use different oil categories and service routines; follow the engine maker's manual and verify the application for your year, make, and model.

Gearcase lubricant

The lower-unit gearcase needs a marine gear-lubricant category matched to the manufacturer specification. Inspect for water intrusion and follow the service interval in the manual.

Fuel-system and storage support

Seasonal boats may need fuel-system maintenance and storage practices in addition to engine oil. Treat these as separate maintenance categories and confirm compatibility before use.

Filters and corrosion protection

Oil filters, fuel filters, and corrosion-protection products vary by engine and environment. Use the official lookup for fitment and application guidance.
