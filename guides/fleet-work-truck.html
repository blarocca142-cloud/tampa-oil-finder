Work truck fleet fluids — category starter guide

A work-truck fleet may include gas and diesel powertrains, towing, idling, payload, and mixed model years. Build a maintenance checklist around fluid categories, then verify each VIN, year, make, and model on AMSOIL.com.

Engine oil by powertrain

Gasoline and diesel engines can require different performance categories and service practices. Keep the vehicle configuration and manufacturer specification attached to each maintenance record.

Transmission and differential

Automatic, manual, and automated transmissions are not interchangeable. Rear axles, front axles, and transfer cases also need their own verified gear-lubricant categories.

Severe-service conditions

Towing, idling, dust, stop-and-go routes, and payload can affect the maintenance schedule. Use the manufacturer's severe-service guidance instead of a generic fleet interval.

Filters and records

Oil, fuel, air, and cabin filters have separate fitments. Record mileage, hours, fluid category, and inspection results so the next service is repeatable.
