Motorcycle synthetics — category starter guide

Motorcycles are not small cars. Shared sumps, wet clutches, high RPM, and storage seasons change what “the right oil” means. Use categories below as a checklist, then verify on AMSOIL.com.

Engine oil (shared-sump vs separate)

Many bikes share engine and transmission oil; others separate them. Wet-clutch designs often need oils meeting motorcycle-specific friction requirements (for example JASO-oriented categories). Confirm your bike’s needs in the official lookup.

Fork / suspension fluid

Fork oil weight affects damping. Match the weight your OEM or suspension tuner specifies; product selection belongs on AMSOIL.com.

Chain, primary, and gearbox (as equipped)

Shaft-drive, chain, belt, and separate gearboxes each have different maintenance categories. Do not assume automotive gear oil is correct without verification.

Filters

Use the motorcycle application on AMSOIL.com to confirm oil filter fitment.
