ATV & UTV fluids — category starter guide

ATVs and UTVs see dust, mud, stop-and-go heat, and high-load low-speed operation. Start with the categories below, then verify exact fitment and specifications for your machine on AMSOIL.com.

Engine oil and shared sump

Many powersports machines share engine, transmission, and clutch oil; others separate these systems. Identify the layout and required friction or performance specifications before selecting an engine-oil category.

Wet clutch considerations

A wet clutch can make friction behavior important. Do not assume passenger-car motor oil is suitable; use the official application lookup and the equipment manual to confirm the required category.

Gearcase and differentials

Front or rear differentials, transaxles, and separate gearcases may use different gear-lubricant categories. Match the specification and capacity to the model.

Air and oil filters

Dusty riding makes filtration and service intervals especially important. Confirm oil-filter fitment and follow the manufacturer's inspection schedule.
