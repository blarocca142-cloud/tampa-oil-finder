Zero-turn mower fluids — category starter guide

Starter guide · Independent AMSOIL Dealer Blayne Larocca · Tampa, FL

Zero-turn mowers combine a small four-stroke engine with hydrostatic drive components that work in heat, dust, and long mowing sessions. Review these fluid categories, then verify the model-specific requirements on AMSOIL.com.

Four-stroke engine oil

Engine oil must match the mower maker's required viscosity and performance category. Check the oil level on the schedule in the manual, especially before long mowing sessions.

Hydrostatic transmission fluid

Hydrostatic drives may require a dedicated transmission or hydraulic-fluid category. Do not substitute engine oil or automotive fluid without verified model guidance.

Filters and cooling surfaces

Engine oil filters, air filters, and cooling screens help manage contamination and heat. Dust and grass buildup can affect service needs, so inspect and clean as directed.

Grease and deck hardware

Spindles, idlers, and chassis points may use grease categories separate from the engine and drive fluids. Confirm grease type and lubrication points in the equipment manual.

Reminder

  • Identify the exact mower model before selecting a fluid category
  • Do not mix hydraulic or hydrostatic fluids without compatibility guidance
